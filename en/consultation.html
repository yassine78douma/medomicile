FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should I call 15?

Call 15 for a life-threatening situation, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, severe injury or a road accident.

Does a home consultation replace emergency care?

No. A home consultation does not replace public emergency services. Call 15 in a life-threatening emergency.

Which areas does Medomicile serve?

Medomicile provides guidance and home consultations in Kenitra and Mehdia, subject to availability.

How do I request a home consultation?

Call Medomicile at +212 6 63 05 82 22 or contact the team on WhatsApp to explain your needs and check availability.
