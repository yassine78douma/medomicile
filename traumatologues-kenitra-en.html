Useful information about trauma and orthopedics

If you are looking for a trauma or orthopedic specialist in Kenitra, these doctors assess fractures, sprains, ligament and tendon injuries, osteoarthritis and conditions affecting the shoulder, knee, hip, hand, foot and spine.

The information shown is for guidance only. Please contact the office directly to confirm the address, opening times, availability and consultation details. Medomicile is an independent local directory and does not rank healthcare professionals.

When should you consult a trauma and orthopedic specialist?

After a fracture, a significant sprain, persistent joint pain, a sports injury or functional discomfort of the shoulder, knee, hip, hand, foot or spine.

What is the difference between a trauma specialist, an orthopedist and a rheumatologist?

A trauma and orthopedic specialist evaluates bone, joint, ligament or tendon injuries, sometimes with surgical care. A rheumatologist mainly follows medical diseases of joints, bones and tendons.

Do you need an X-ray before consulting?

Not always. Depending on the pain or injury, the doctor may request an X-ray or another imaging exam after the clinical assessment.

Which specialist should you see after a sprain or fracture?

A trauma and orthopedic specialist can provide a specialist opinion, especially if pain persists, walking is difficult or there is visible deformity.

How can you find a private trauma specialist in Kenitra?

Use this list, compare districts and call the office directly to confirm the address, availability and consultation details.

Does a trauma specialist treat sports injuries?

Yes, many trauma and orthopedic specialists assess sprains, ligament injuries, tendon pain and sport-related trauma.

In case of severe pain after trauma, deformity, inability to walk, loss of sensation or a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately.
