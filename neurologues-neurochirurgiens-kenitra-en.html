Useful information about neurology and neurosurgery

This directory helps patients find contact details for private neurologists and neurosurgeons practicing in Kenitra. The information is provided for orientation and may change.

Neurology may concern migraines, dizziness, epilepsy, memory disorders, neurological pain, and diseases of the nerves, brain, spinal cord and muscles. Neurosurgery evaluates conditions that may require specialist surgical advice or procedures, especially involving the brain, spine and spinal cord.

Medomicile is a professional directory. It does not replace medical advice, does not make diagnoses and does not book appointments for these offices. Always contact the practice to confirm the address, opening hours and consultation terms.

In case of sudden speech disorder, paralysis, loss of consciousness, seizures, serious trauma or suspected stroke, contact emergency services immediately.
